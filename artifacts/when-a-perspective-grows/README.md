## Question and intended use

What warrants treating a measured relation as experiential and carrying that interpretation across learning? Readers can inspect the philosophical argument and reproduce its finite mathematical examples. This is a philosophical working paper, not a new empirical study.

## Claim supported

The paper offers a critical synthesis for objective phenomenology: distinguish the phenomenal target, the evidence for attributing it at each stage, and the strength of the resulting comparison. If a common target is specified and measurements at both stages are warranted against it, results can support a local endpoint comparison without first assuming complete phenomenal identity. Where the target or its measurement remains unsupported, statistical alignment cannot supply the missing interpretation. The contribution is a critical synthesis with worked examples, not a new validation method.

The v2.4 revision adds a bounded autobiographical-memory illustration and situates it against established response-shift, longitudinal-measurement and memory discussions. These sources do not verify a past phenomenal episode, establish that a remembered experience changed, or show that retrospective access improved. Memory remains an illustrative evidential complication, not a result of this paper.

The calculations establish only properties of the stipulated geometry and conditional choice model. They do not establish that real experiences have the assumed structure or that the model’s conditions can be independently validated.

## Method and reproduction

Requires Python 3 with its standard library. From this folder run:

```sh
python3 reproduce.py
```

The script checks all 120 ordered injective placements using squared distances, so no floating-point tolerance is needed. It checks the equal old-point profiles of n and m and their different distances to z. Endpoint calculations use exact rational arithmetic. The mixture functions are affine in w, so their endpoint values determine the ranges for 0 ≤ w ≤ 0.1.

Expected output:

```text
Ordered injective placements: 120
Exact metric placements: 1
Ordinal placements: 16
n/m squared-distance profiles to a,b,c: (4, 5, 13)
Squared distances n-z, m-z: (10, 18)
Continuity range: 0.90–0.91; reversal range: 0.10–0.19
Stipulated mathematics only; no experiential or empirical validation.
```

Noncomputational inspection: examine the paper’s inference from stable reports to preserved experience; compare §1 with Negro, §§2–3 with Nagel/Lee/Isaac and §7 with the stated objections. Assess whether the conditional example adds a useful requirement beyond ordinary measurement validation. Do not infer philosophical soundness from a successful script run.

## Limitations and strongest objection

The distinction between changed experience and changed judgement remains unresolved where the needed constraints cannot be supported independently. Agreement across methods can reflect a shared decision process. The hypothetical does not supply a validated procedure separating that process from experience. The geometry is constructed, not phenomenal data; endpoint preservation does not establish uninterrupted invariance. Meredith’s full-text comparison remains incomplete. The v2.4 memory example does not establish the accuracy, preservation or phenomenal character of past experience. The paper establishes no new general measurement method or theory of consciousness.
