## The note’s inference, separated from those sources

The note reconstructs two requirements: standards supporting a warranted a priori exclusion of relevant blameless disagreement, and a warranted practice locating error. These are analytical connections identified through Shapiro and Pantsar, not premises demonstrated by the matching studies. Shared capacities, agreement rates or one error diagnosis do not alone establish both. The reply assumes shared content and justified checking; it leaves the authority of those standards open.

The base-conversion illustration is elementary valuation: thirteen equals 1 × 10 + 3 and 2 × 5 + 3. It illustrates value preservation, not identical concepts or equal cognitive ease. No computational evidence package is required for this non-computational note.

## Prior work, scope and omitted material

The artifact adds a focused reconstruction and unresolved question, not a new empirical result, a demonstrated refutation, a comprehensive originality survey or a novel foundation. The closest account examined is Pantsar’s; Shapiro supplies the criterion and checking case. The bibliography is the six works above. The wider research record, private author discussions, model transcripts, submission materials and downloaded source papers are not part of this release. No synthetic dataset, reproduction claim or field-wide priority claim is made.
